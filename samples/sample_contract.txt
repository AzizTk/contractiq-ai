Each party shall protect confidential information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to personnel and advisers who need to know.

4. Indemnity
Provider shall indemnify Customer against any and all claims, losses, and expenses arising in connection with the services.

5. Limitation of liability
Provider's liability under this agreement is unlimited and without limitation for all claims arising from the services.

6. Term and renewal
The initial term ends on 31 December 2026. This agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party gives 90 days' written notice.

7. Termination
Customer may terminate this agreement at any time in its sole discretion by written notice.

8. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

9. General
Provider shall return Customer materials upon termination. Each party must notify the other of a material security incident without undue delay.
